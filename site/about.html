Our Local Story

Built on Trust in Vallejo, California Homes

Plumbing problems can disrupt your home, slow down your business, and quickly lead to costly damage when they are not handled properly. Rickys Plumbing provides dependable solutions designed to get your plumbing system working correctly again. Since 2020, we have served residential and commercial customers with professional workmanship, careful attention to detail, and a commitment to high-quality jobs that are clean and well done. As a trusted plumbing contractor in Vallejo, CA, we approach every project with the same goal: solve the problem efficiently and leave our customers with reliable results. Our slogan, “Emergencies solved instantly,” reflects our focus on being ready when plumbing trouble cannot wait.

We handle a complete range of plumbing services for homes and businesses. When you need a plumber for plumbing repair or emergency plumbing, we can diagnose the issue and provide the right solution. Our services include drain cleaning, snake services, camera service, and help with sewer clogs or drain stoppage problems. We also provide sewer line repair, sewer line replacement, leak detection, pipe repair, and pipe replacement for damaged or aging plumbing systems. For hot water needs, we offer water heater repair, water heater installation, and tankless water heater installation. Whether the problem is easy to spot or hidden behind walls or underground, we focus on finding the source and completing the work correctly. Our additional services include toilet repair, toilet installation, faucet repair, garbage disposal repair, and sump pump installation. We also provide commercial plumbing services for businesses that need dependable plumbing systems and prompt attention when problems arise. We understand the importance of clean work areas, professional service, and repairs or installations that are built to last.

From routine plumbing repair to urgent sewer, drain, pipe, and water heater problems, you can trust that your plumbing will run smoothly with Ricky's Plumbing. Contact us today for professional plumbing service in Vallejo, CA.
